# 💰 Stock Market Beginner Roadmap

> A 4-week learning guide for understanding how stocks, markets, and investing work — before advancing to options or more complex trading.

───────────────────────────────────────────────
## 🗓️ WEEK 1 — Foundations: What Are Stocks?

**Goal:** Understand what stocks are, how they’re traded, and why prices move.

### 📖 Study
- [ ] Read: [Investopedia — What Is a Stock?](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/s/stock.asp)  
- [ ] Watch: [Graham Stephan — How the Stock Market Works for Beginners](https://www.youtube.com/@GrahamStephan)  
- [ ] Read: *The Little Book of Common Sense Investing* — John C. Bogle (Ch. 1–3)

### 🧩 Learn Concepts
- [ ] What a company share represents  
- [ ] Stock exchanges (NYSE, NASDAQ)  
- [ ] Market orders vs. limit orders  
- [ ] Tickers, bid/ask, volume  
- [ ] How dividends work  

### 🧪 Practice
- [ ] Create a watchlist of 5 companies you actually know  
- [ ] Track their daily price moves for one week  
- [ ] Note big jumps and search *why* they happened  

### ✅ Checkpoint
> You can explain what a share of stock actually is, and what makes its price go up or down.

───────────────────────────────────────────────
## ⚙️ WEEK 2 — Investing vs. Trading

**Goal:** Learn the difference between long-term investing and short-term trading.

### 📖 Study
- [ ] Watch: [Ben Felix — Investing Basics](https://www.youtube.com/@BenFelix)  
- [ ] Read: [Morningstar Guide to Investing Styles](https://www.morningstar.com/)  
- [ ] Skim: *A Random Walk Down Wall Street* — Burton Malkiel (Intro chapters)

### 🧩 Learn Concepts
- [ ] Fundamental Analysis vs. Technical Analysis  
- [ ] Value vs. Growth stocks  
- [ ] Diversification and index funds  
- [ ] ETFs vs. mutual funds  
- [ ] Long-term compounding  

### 🧪 Practice
- [ ] Open a free paper trading account (TD, Webull, Investopedia)  
- [ ] Buy mock positions in:
  - 1 individual stock  
  - 1 ETF (e.g. SPY or VOO)  
- [ ] Track how each behaves over the week  

### ✅ Checkpoint
> You know whether you prefer long-term investing, short-term trading, or a mix.

───────────────────────────────────────────────
## 💹 WEEK 3 — Reading the Market

**Goal:** Learn to interpret charts, news, and company performance.

### 📖 Study
- [ ] Read: [Investopedia — How to Read Stock Charts](https://www.investopedia.com/articles/technical/102201.asp)  
- [ ] Watch: [The Plain Bagel — How to Analyze a Company](https://www.youtube.com/@ThePlainBagel)  
- [ ] Read company investor relations pages (e.g. Apple, Tesla)

### 🧩 Learn Concepts
- [ ] Candlestick charts, trends, support/resistance  
- [ ] Volume and momentum indicators  
- [ ] Earnings reports (EPS, revenue, guidance)  
- [ ] Market news and sentiment  

### 🧪 Practice
- [ ] Pick one stock  
- [ ] Look at 1-month, 6-month, and 1-year charts  
- [ ] Identify one uptrend, one downtrend, and explain possible reasons  
- [ ] Read the company’s latest quarterly report summary  

### ✅ Checkpoint
> You can glance at a stock chart and understand what it’s showing — not just numbers and lines.

───────────────────────────────────────────────
## 🧠 WEEK 4 — Building a Portfolio & Managing Risk

**Goal:** Learn how to allocate, protect, and grow your investments responsibly.

### 📖 Study
- [ ] Watch: [Andrei Jikh — How to Build a Stock Portfolio](https://www.youtube.com/@AndreiJikh)  
- [ ] Read: [Vanguard’s Investor Education Center](https://investor.vanguard.com/investor-resources-education)  
- [ ] Skim: *The Psychology of Money* — Morgan Housel  

### 🧩 Learn Concepts
- [ ] Asset allocation & diversification  
- [ ] Risk tolerance and time horizon  
- [ ] Reinvesting dividends  
- [ ] Dollar-cost averaging  
- [ ] Avoiding emotional decisions  

### 🧪 Practice
- [ ] Build a mock portfolio with 3–5 positions  
  - Large-cap stock  
  - Growth stock  
  - ETF or index fund  
- [ ] Track weekly performance  
- [ ] Note how you *feel* during gains/losses (psychology matters)

### ✅ Checkpoint
> You understand how to build a balanced, long-term portfolio and avoid rookie mistakes.

───────────────────────────────────────────────

- [ ] Read: *One Up on Wall Street* — Peter Lynch  
- [ ] Learn: Tax basics for investing (capital gains, dividends)
